Rethinking Institutional Protection & Social Resilience

The Care Governance Deficit presents an architectural blueprint for governments, international NGOs, and social policy planners. It addresses the systemic gap in elder care, child safeguarding, and demographic welfare transitions across low- and middle-income countries.

  • ✔ Theoretical and mathematical frameworks for the Geriatric Dignity Index (GDI).
  • ✔ Implementation models for child safeguarding and institutional dignity audits.
  • ✔ Telemetry models for sub-Saharan African and Middle Eastern social protection systems.

From principle to audit

Dignity is usually treated as a value that cannot be checked. We turn it into indicators that an institution can observe, report and improve, in four steps.

  1. Assess

    Older people, or their carers on their behalf, complete a short assessment across eight dignity domains.

  2. Audit

    We check the institution's policies, staffing and complaint routes against the same eight domains.

  3. Act

    The gap between the two becomes a short, prioritised action plan the provider can own.

  4. Re-assess

    Repeat the assessment on a set schedule to show whether dignity has improved.

The experience-governance gap. A care home can keep complete records and still leave residents feeling unheard. Measuring what people experience next to what the institution has in place shows where the gap is, and who is responsible for closing it.

Services

Early engagements are pilots. We work closely with a small number of providers and agencies while the instruments are being validated.

Dignity audits

A structured review of how older people experience care in your service, and what your organisation has in place to protect their dignity.

  • Assessment with the Geriatric Dignity Index, using self-report, assisted or proxy pathways
  • Review of policies, supervision, safeguarding and complaint routes
  • Experience-governance gap report and a prioritised action plan

Staff training

Practical sessions for care workers, supervisors and managers on delivering dignity-centred care and using dignity data in day-to-day practice.

  • Respect, consent and communication with older people, including those with cognitive impairment
  • Recognising and responding to safeguarding concerns
  • Using assessment results in supervision and team meetings

Governance advisory

Support for agencies and social protection programmes that want dignity written into their monitoring and accountability arrangements.

  • Indicator design and monitoring frameworks
  • Policy briefs and technical notes
  • Pilot design and evaluation planning

We also work on care facilitation and case management, safeguarding and psychosocial support, applied social research, family and caregiver support, and digital care solutions, through partnerships across Africa and beyond.

Publications & Frameworks

The instruments below are research tools under development. They are not validated scales and should not be used for high-stakes decisions about individuals.

Geriatric Dignity Index (GDI) v0.2

Eight domains of dignity in later-life care, a 16-item short field form, and four assessment pathways: self-report, assisted, proxy and unable to assess. Provisional, in field testing in Nigeria and Oman.

In field testing
Caregiver Resilience Index (CRI)

A planned companion measure of the strain, support and resilience of family and professional caregivers.

In development
Developmental Agency Index (DAI)

A planned measure of agency, participation and developmental opportunity for people who depend on care.

In development
Digital Care Telemetry

An emerging approach that links field-level care signals, measurement, dashboards, action and reassessment.

Emerging
Care Governance Architecture (CGA)

The framework that links dignity measurement to institutional responsibility, reporting and action.
